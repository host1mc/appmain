About ENDHOST

Last updated: August 12, 2026

ENDHOST is a free hosting service. It does two things: it runs code you wrote — Node.js, Python, Ruby, Go, PHP or Bun, in a container we supervise, whether that is a Discord bot, a Telegram bot, a web service or a scheduled job — and it runs a managed status bot for your Minecraft server, keeping a live embed updated in your Discord channel with player count, online or offline state, version, and MOTD. Either way you host nothing yourself.

Why we built it

Every Minecraft community ends up answering the same question in chat: "is the server up?" The usual answers are a bot you have to host on a VPS you pay for, a paid status service, or a member refreshing a website. All three cost somebody money or attention.

Running one small status bot does not need a server of its own. One process can keep thousands of embeds current, because checking a Minecraft server and editing a Discord message is cheap work. So we built the shared version: you configure your bot in a web panel, we run it on our infrastructure, and the embed in your Discord stays current around the clock.

What the service actually does

  • Polls your server. We query your Minecraft server's public status endpoint on a schedule — Java or Bedrock — and read back the live player count, MOTD, version, and reachability.
  • Keeps one embed current. Instead of spamming a channel, your bot edits a single message in place, so your status channel stays clean and the history is not full of duplicates.
  • Lets you design the card. Colours, title, thumbnail, field layout, and the wording for the online and offline states are all yours to set in the builder. No template lock-in.
  • Handles auto-replies. You can set keyword replies so your bot answers common questions ("ip", "version", "how to join") without a moderator having to.
  • Runs unattended. Once configured, there is nothing to restart, patch, or pay for. If your Minecraft server goes down, the embed flips to offline and flips back on its own.

How it is free

The hosting itself is genuinely free — no paid tier gating the features above. The running costs are covered by advertising on the public pages of this site, which is why you will see ad slots on the marketing and panel pages. That is the entire business model; we do not sell accounts, upsell a premium plan, or sell your personal information. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what advertising partners can and cannot see.

How your data is handled

Your Discord bot token is the one credential that genuinely matters, so it is encrypted before it is stored and only ever decrypted in memory by the process that posts your embeds. It is never sent back to your browser, never displayed again after you save it, and never written to a log. The rest of what we store is the minimum needed to run your bot — your Minecraft server address, your Discord channel, your embed design, and your account details.

We also record a device fingerprint and IP address at signup. That exists to stop one person from farming hundreds of accounts on a free service, not to profile you. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Who runs this

ENDHOST is built and operated by Endevil, a small independent project rather than a company with a support department. Support, feature requests, and outage reports all go through the same place: our Discord community, where the people who actually write the code read them. If you would rather not use Discord, the contact page lists the alternative.

Roadmap and honesty about limits

Because this is a free shared service, there are limits worth stating plainly. We do not promise an uptime figure, we can suspend accounts that abuse the platform, and a very large Minecraft network with unusual requirements is probably better served by hosting its own bot. What we do commit to is that the core hosting stays free, that your token stays encrypted, and that you can delete your account and its data whenever you want.

Get started

Creating a bot takes a few minutes: follow the setup guide, or make a free account and paste your token. If you get stuck, the FAQ on the home page and the Discord cover the rest.
