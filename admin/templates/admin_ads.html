Ad Networks

The page-head loaders and the site's ad providers (Monetag MultiTag, Adstera…). A network that is off emits no loader tag for anyone. When every network is off, no advertising can work at all — every zone below resolves off for everyone, whatever its switch says. Turning the master off below kills the loaders too.

Consent & Ad-Block Guard

Ads do not depend on visitor consent, and a visitor running an ad blocker is sent to /blocked until it is off — search crawlers excepted, always treated as off.

Per-Page Switches

Which pages may carry advertising at all. A page switch and a zone switch are independent axes: both must be on for an ad to render. Turning a page on does not make a zone appear, and a zone that is on renders nothing on a page that is off.

The account-page defaults reproduce a denylist that used to be hardcoded in the site's code: those six pages handle credentials, account data, bot tokens and reply content, so they ship off. Turning one on is a deliberate act.

How It Resolves

A visitor's ads are decided by six switches, first "no" wins:

  1. Global master — this page. Off means nothing renders for anyone.
  2. Per-user "All Ads" toggle — set from the user's detail page (Advertising section). Off kills everything for that user.
  3. Ad network switch — the head loaders, this page. When every network is off, no zone renders for anyone.
  4. Per-page switch — this page. The page the visitor is on must be on.
  5. Global per-zone switch — this page. The zone must be on.
  6. Per-user per-zone override — set from the user's detail page.

Page and zone are independent axes, not a hierarchy — both must be on. Turning a page on does not make a zone appear, and a zone that is on renders nothing on a page that is off.

Consent does not affect whether ads render.

Ad-block guard always gates a blocked visitor to /blocked, except search crawlers, which are treated as off.

Anonymous visitors only see 1, 3, 4 and 5. Per-user overrides live in the Users list → select a user.
